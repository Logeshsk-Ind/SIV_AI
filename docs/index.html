WHY SIV-AI
01

Wavelet-Guided Architecture

A 2D Discrete Wavelet Transform decomposes the degraded image into LL, LH, HL, and HH sub-bands. SIV-AI restores low- and high-frequency information through dedicated neural pathways before inverse-wavelet reconstruction.

SUB-BAND FEATURE SEPARATION
02

2× Resolution Recovery

Converts 128×128 low-resolution sensor inputs into 256×256 restored images while recovering fine structural information without introducing artificial features.

03

GPU-Accelerated

PyTorch-based inference is optimized for NVIDIA GPU execution, enabling low-latency restoration suitable for inspection-line workflows.

04

Edge-Preserving

A structure-aware restoration objective preserves sharp boundaries, line patterns, and high-frequency details critical to semiconductor image analysis.
